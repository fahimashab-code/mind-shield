LOCAL BY DESIGN

No account. No uploads.

Mind Shield stores domains, time totals, settings, and session timestamps. It does not collect page contents, typed text, or passwords. Usage history is kept for 90 days; the last 100 completed Shield sessions are saved.

Website access lets Chrome redirect your blocked sites. Tab access identifies the current domain. Idle detection pauses the tracker. Alarms save usage periodically. Storage remembers your settings.

Clearing statistics keeps your blocked list and active Shield timer. Removing the extension deletes its local data.

What the timers mean

Protected time is elapsed time with Shield enabled, including time when Chrome is closed. Usage time estimates active foreground browsing. Background tabs, other applications, incognito windows, and Chrome internal pages are excluded.

Usage is saved about every 30 seconds. Sleep, delayed alarms, crashes, or idle detection can cause small differences. Shield works in this Chrome profile; Chrome itself still lets you disable or remove extensions.
